# eat-your-fiber

**Singular learning theory on—and at the boundary of—zero-loss fibers: Lean 4 kernel certificates paired with PyTorch empiricism.**

Different parameter vectors computing the *exact same function* can sit in valleys with macroscopically different local learning coefficients $(\lambda, m_{\log})$—and in higher-order multilinear models, the zero-loss fiber can disappear to infinity entirely while $\inf_\theta L(\theta) = 0$. Building on [residual-slt-in-a-haystack](https://github.com/bubskee/residual-slt-in-a-haystack) and [slt-bridge](https://github.com/bubskee/slt-bridge), this repository studies two complementary regimes side by side, aiming for macroscopic $\Delta\lambda$ phenomena that are simultaneously **kernel-certified in Lean 4** and **measured live in PyTorch**:

1. **Order-2 — Inside the zero-loss fiber (LoRA / Reduced-Rank / Self-Attention):** The rank-$\le k$ matrix locus is Zariski-closed, so every realizable target $M^*$ has a nonempty finite zero-loss fiber $\{BA = M^*\}$. Across that fiber, $(\lambda, m_{\log})$ undergoes an exact **plateau-to-parabola phase transition** (flat Aoyagi–Watanabe plateau for $q \le q_c$, exact parabola $\lambda_{\mathrm{AW}}^{\mathrm{even}} + \frac{1}{2}(q - q_c)^2$ for $q > q_c$—a rise of $\frac{1}{2}(q - q_c)^2$ above the plateau for even $m+n+k$, $\frac{1}{8}$ less for odd—placing standard LoRA initialization $q = k$ at the top of the parabola). In single-head self-attention (Frobenius weight-product loss), nonlinear composition creates a sharp **architectural coupling dichotomy**: joint `V+O` quadruples the supercritical jump to $2(q - H/2)_+^2$, whereas joint `Q+K` develops an all-orders $GL(q)$ gauge symmetry that **completely annihilates** the parabola (before multi-head transversality decouples both pairs). Both reappear at the supercritical tops inside a real pretrained 2-layer causal Transformer (subcritical points sit below theory at finite $\beta$), where iso-functional fiber surgery also separates plasticity from stability and splits late-stage LLC drops into functional change vs. pure fiber sliding. A faithful 500k-step, exact-Table-2 SGLD rerun of Hoogland et al.'s in-context regression Transformer traces its late LLC drop (`100.9% ± 1.6%`) to isotropic localization following one exact `LN_0 ↔ W_QKV` rescaling orbit—confirmed directly by exact Gauss–Newton spectra—shows Hitchcock & Hoogland's RMSProp-pSGLD (`arXiv:2507.21449`) still drops `-65.0`, and eliminates the drop (`-0.27 ± 0.83`, zero weight edits) via an exact orbit-covariant localization metric.
2. **Order-3 — Boundary of the zero-loss fiber (Bilinear MLPs / 3-Tensors):** For width-$k$ bilinear models fitted entrywise to a 3-tensor, $T(A,B,C) = \sum_r a_r \otimes b_r \otimes c_r$ (a single-input bilinear MLP $x \mapsto W_{\mathrm{out}}((W_1 x) \odot (W_2 x))$ only sees the input-symmetrized part), the real rank-$\le k$ locus is **not closed**. When $T^*$ is a real limit of rank-$\le k$ tensors but $\mathrm{rk}(T^*) > k$ (e.g. $W$ at $k = 2$), $\inf_\theta L(\theta) = 0$ yet the finite zero-loss fiber is **empty**. Leveraging vendored 3-tensor border-rank formalizations from [`openai/math`](https://github.com/openai/math), we certify that under a Gaussian prior ($\ell_2$ weight decay), the canonical $W$-tensor forces a non-compact SLT universality class—**stretched-exponential free energy** $-\log Z_W^{(2)}(N) \asymp N^{1/7}$—strictly between realizable Watanabe $\Theta(\log N)$ and compact unrealizable $\Theta(N)$. $W$ sits on the measure-zero hyperdeterminant wall; on the **positive-measure** open set $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ (probability $1 - \pi/4 \approx 21.46\%$ for Gaussian $2\times 2\times 2$ targets), where no best rank-2 approximation exists and $\inf L = L_{\mathrm{wall}} > 0$ is never attained, a second fractional class appears: $\Delta L \asymp R^{-6}$, $\inf(\Delta L + \gamma\|\theta\|_2^2) \asymp \gamma^{3/4}$ (with near-minimizer $\Delta L^* \asymp \gamma^{3/4}$, $R^2 \asymp \gamma^{-1/4}$), and $-\log Z - \beta L_{\mathrm{wall}} \asymp \beta^{1/4}$. All three two-sided laws are now Lean-certified (`[LC]`) both for general $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ targets under strengthened `GenericWallHyp` + $\mathrm{GL}(2)^3$ wall attainment AND unconditionally (zero hypotheses) on an explicit positive-Lebesgue-measure open ball `t3Ball` around $T_3$ (`volume t3Ball ≥ (1/1024)^8 > 0`).

## Scope, assumptions & honest limitations

1. **Non-compact prior vs. Watanabe's compact parameter space (Track 1):** Standard SLT assumes compact prior support $\Theta \subset \mathbb{R}^P$; on any compact ball $\|\theta\|_2 \le R$, width-2 $W$-tensor fitting is strictly unrealizable ($L^*(R) \asymp R^{-12} > 0$) with linear free energy $-\log Z \sim N L^*(R)$. The stretched-exponential class $-\log Z_W^{(2)}(N) \asymp N^{1/7}$ specifically requires a **non-compact Gaussian prior** ($\ell_2$ weight decay); general tails $e^{-c R^\alpha}$ give $N^{\alpha/(12+\alpha)}$, and heavy-tailed priors $R^{-\nu}$ yield prior-dependent $\Theta(\log N)$ scaling (see [`notes/bilinear_border_rank.md`](notes/bilinear_border_rank.md) §2.4).
2. **Formalization boundaries on Order-2 fibers (Track 2):**
   - Complete $(r^*, p, q)$ RRR fiber stratification for arbitrary invertible active block $D \in GL(r^*, \mathbb{R})$ (`RRRFiberStratification.lean`) and single-head `Q+K` flat cancellation for arbitrary orthogonal bases $U, V \in O(d)$ (`AttentionLoRA.lean`) are **`[LC]`**.
   - Non-orthogonal `V+O` row-space overlap (`q_eff = rank([A_{O,0}; A_{V,0}])`), multi-head decoupling ($n_{\mathrm{heads}} \ge 2$), and nonlinear MLP pullback invariance through a non-degenerate Gauss-Newton Fisher $\mathcal{H} \succ 0$ remain `[paper]` + `[empirical]` ([`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md)).
3. **Low-sample noisy fine-tuning nuance:** When finite noisy training data ($S = 48, \sigma = 0.25$) excites all $H$ LoRA ranks into fitting noise, there is no inactive zero-loss fiber ($k_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0$) and moderate decoupled AdamW shrinks $\|A_{\perp B}\|_F$ without improving test MSE; strong AdamW, coupled SGD+WD, or subcritical rank-$2$ initialization cut test MSE by $2\text{--}8\times$. Moreover, raw centered empirical LLC turns strongly negative (`-314.7`) at the generalizing rank-2 saddle, illustrating why functional KL LLC is essential ([`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md) §2.2).
4. **Finite-$\beta$ estimation & sampler choice:** Across all empirical sweeps, Metropolis-adjusted samplers (MALA / preconditioned MALA / SMC+HMC) are used because unadjusted SGLD incurs severe upward discretization bias on stiff supercritical directions (`36.81` to `54.54` vs. `32.00` on GELU MLP; [`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md) §1.3). On singular subcritical plateaus ($q < q_c$), finite-$\beta$ MALA retains the expected negative $O(1/\log(\beta/\gamma))$ offset that closes monotonically as $\beta/\gamma$ grows.
5. **Generic border-rank formalization scope & SwiGLU regimes (Track 1):** All three two-sided generic border-rank laws ($R^{-6}$, $\gamma^{3/4}$, $\beta^{1/4}$) are now `[LC]` both for general targets under strengthened `GenericWallHyp` + $\mathrm{GL}(2)^3$ wall attainment AND unconditionally (zero hypotheses) on the positive-Lebesgue-measure open ball `t3Ball` around $T_3$ (`t3Ball_twoSided_laws`, `volume_t3Ball_pos`), along with near-minimizer $\Delta L^* \asymp \gamma^{3/4}$ and $R^2 \asymp \gamma^{-1/4}$ (`generic_nearMinimizer_scaling`). Verifying `GenericWallHyp` + $\mathrm{GL}(2)^3$ attainment on *all* of $\{\operatorname{Det} < 0\}$ outside `t3Ball`, the virial $\to 3$ limit, and the $\frac{33}{8}$, $\frac{15}{4}$ Laplace offsets remain `[paper]`. The $77.5\text{--}90\%$ rates at $d = 4$ come from an L-BFGS degeneracy detector on 40 targets per $(d,k)$, not an exact classification. On a bilinear target ($d = 4 \to 4$; Bilinear width 4 fails, width 6 realizes), under-width SwiGLU escapes by gate linearization ($W_1 \to 0$; predicted $\mathrm{MSE}^* \asymp \gamma^{2/3}$, virial $2$): at width 6 we measured virial `2.02` but MSE slope `0.742`, so the exponent is only roughly confirmed. On one SwiGLU-native $d = 3$ teacher, widths 2–4 keep bounded norms (norm² $\le 33.3$) across $\gamma \in [10^{-5}, 10^{-1}]$, suggesting the escape comes from bilinear/quadratic sub-circuits rather than SwiGLU per se (one teacher tested) ([`notes/generic_border_rank.md`](notes/generic_border_rank.md) §3.4).
6. **Pre-LN vs. Pre-RMSNorm (Track 2):** Pre-LN's mean-centering puts the rank-$d$ family $\mathbf{1}_d u^\top$ in the Fisher kernel of residual-stream writes, dropping $M_{\mathrm{eff}}$ to $d - 1$ and breaking $p \leftrightarrow q$ symmetry; Pre-RMSNorm has no such kernel and stays symmetric. The derivation is `[paper]`; it was checked on one $3{,}240$-parameter Pre-LN twin that only partly learned the pretraining task (recall `0.46`, vs. `1.00` for the Pre-RMSNorm model).
7. **Optimizer dependence of fiber locking (Track 2, $d_{\mathrm{model}} = 12$ Transformer, 5 seeds):** From the exact $(0,0)$ point both SGD and Adam stay near rank 2. From a realistic near-plateau start $(0,0) + 10^{-3}$ ($\max\lvert\Delta\mathrm{logit}\rvert = 6.2\times 10^{-4}$), SGD stays locked (recall `0.253`, update effective rank `2.41`), but Adam's $1/\sqrt{v}$ normalization breaks the saddle (`0.595`, `3.53`), matching the supercritical checkpoints, so the plasticity–stability split largely washes out under Adam on non-directional tasks. With $\mathrm{wd} = 0$, LoRA+ ($\eta_B = 12\,\eta_A$) ends deeper in the $A$-supercritical cone than equal-LR Adam ($(p_{\mathrm{eff}}, q_{\mathrm{eff}}) = (0.00, 6.73)$, $\hat\lambda = $ `61.47 ± 0.46` vs. $(0.24, 5.57)$, `58.44 ± 0.53`), because the small $\eta_A$ freezes $A_{\mathrm{in}}$ at its full-rank init. Extrapolation to large Transformers with stochastic schedules is `[conjectural]`.
8. **DevInterp bridge scope & honest caveats:** Our faithful 500k exact-FP32 rerun reproduces Hoogland et al.'s peak (`46,003`), `LR3` (`126,262`), trough (`363,636`), and `LR5` uptick (`r = 0.981` vs. `llcs.pt`), though our raw trough is ~29 points deeper (`92.2` vs. `121.2`) and their original S3 checkpoints remain private. The finding is about the finite-$\gamma$ localized estimator: both `RAW` and `ORBIT` are finite-$\gamma$ estimates (not the asymptotic $\gamma \to 0$ LLC), so we cannot rule out a subtle true-LLC shift over `46k → 364k`. Final-LN collapse and OOD changes still occur; the `AdamW` intervention changes the learned functions (`ID MSE 1.943` vs. `1.804`); and extending this to their attention-only language models (`LM1`–`LM5`) or other DevInterp curves is `[conjectural]` ([`notes/devinterp_fiber_decomposition.md`](notes/devinterp_fiber_decomposition.md) §8, [`notes/devinterp_for_authors.md`](notes/devinterp_for_authors.md)).

## Relation to SLT & ML literature

- **Watanabe (2009, 2018):** *Algebraic Geometry and Statistical Learning Theory* (CUP) and *Mathematical Theory of Bayesian Statistics* (CRC) — foundational SLT free-energy asymptotics, RLCTs $(\lambda, m)$, and WBIC.
- **Aoyagi & Watanabe (2005):** *"Stochastic complexities of reduced rank regression in Bayesian estimation,"* *Neural Networks* 18(7): 924–933 — Theorem 1 gives the exact RLCTs of matrix factorization, here unconditionally mechanized in Lean 4 via elementary projective blowups (`aoyagi_watanabe_rank0`).
- **Aoyagi (2024):** *"Consideration on the learning efficiency of multiple-layered neural networks with linear units,"* *Neural Networks* — global RLCTs of deep linear networks.
- **Lau, Furman, Wang, Murfet & Wei (2025):** *"The Local Learning Coefficient: A Singularity-Aware Complexity Measure,"* AISTATS 2025 ([`arXiv:2308.12108`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12108)) and **Furman & Lau (2024)** ([`arXiv:2402.03698`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03698); withdrawn after being merged into `2308.12108`) — local learning coefficient theory and scalable estimation on deep linear and neural networks; our Track 2 maps exact local LLC phase transitions *within* a fixed-function zero-loss fiber.
- **Treisman (2025/2026):** [`ztreisman/arc-llc`](https://github.com/ztreisman/arc-llc) — arc-space computation of local RLCTs for reduced-rank regression via Smith normal form over $\mathbb{C}[[t]]$ and Docampo's formula, proposing the $(r^*, a, b)$ local RLCT formula as a candidate solution to **MAIS-O70**; our [`RRRFiberStratification.lean`](lean/EatYourFiber/RRRFiberStratification.lean) certifies this formula unconditionally over $\mathbb{R}$ in Lean 4 and proves its equivalence to minimizing irreducible fiber-component codimensions.
- **Hoogland, Wang, Farrugia-Roberts, Carroll, Wei & Murfet (2024/2025):** *"Loss Landscape Degeneracy and Stagewise Development in Transformers,"* to appear in TMLR ([`arXiv:2402.02364`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02364); v1 titled *"The Developmental Landscape of In-Context Learning"*) — reads developmental stages off LLC curves; our late-stage $\Delta\hat\lambda$ decomposition into functional change vs. iso-functional fiber sliding is a check one can run before attributing an LLC drop to a stage. On a retrain of their `L2H4Minf` regression model, the LR3–LR4 drop in the SGLD estimate disappears at function-identical balanced points and tracks the `LN_0 ↔ W_QKV` rescaling orbit; their early stages survive the check.
- **Hitchcock & Hoogland (2025):** *"From Global to Local: A Scalable Benchmark for Local Posterior Sampling"* ([`arXiv:2507.21449`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21449)) — benchmarks local samplers and favours RMSProp-preconditioned SGLD; we test their exact Alg. G.3 `RMSPropSGLD` on our 500k `L2H4Minf` checkpoints and find it still drops `-65.02 ± 4.09` (same isotropic localized posterior), whereas replacing the isotropic prior with an exact orbit-covariant localization metric removes the drop (`-0.27 ± 0.83`).
- **Hu et al. (2022):** *"LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models,"* ICLR 2022 ([`arXiv:2106.09685`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)) — standard asymmetric $(B=0, A \sim \mathcal{N})$ initialization sits at the supercritical top $q = k$.
- **Hayou, Ghosh & Yu (2024):** *"LoRA+: Efficient Low Rank Adaptation of Large Models,"* ICML 2024, PMLR 235: 17783–17806 ([`arXiv:2402.12354`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12354)) — separate learning rates $\eta_B > \eta_A$; in our Transformer runs ($\mathrm{wd} = 0$) this leaves LoRA+ deeper in the $A$-supercritical cone.
- **de Silva & Lim (2008):** *"Tensor rank and the ill-posedness of the best low-rank approximation problem,"* *SIAM J. Matrix Anal. Appl.* 30(3): 1084–1127 ([`arXiv:math/0607647`](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607647)) — border rank, non-closedness of the rank-$\le k$ tensor locus, and a positive-volume set of real $2\times 2\times 2$ tensors with no best rank-2 approximation; the origin of our generic $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ class.
- **Bergqvist (2013):** *"Exact probabilities for typical ranks of $2\times 2\times 2$ and $3\times 3\times 2$ tensors,"* *Linear Algebra Appl.* 438(2): 663–667 — a real Gaussian $2\times 2\times 2$ tensor has rank 3 with probability exactly $1 - \pi/4$, the measure of our generic border-rank class.
- **Sharkey (2023)** ([`arXiv:2305.03452`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03452)) & **Pearce, Dooms, Rigg, Oramas & Sharkey (2024)** ([`arXiv:2410.08417`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08417)) — bilinear MLPs and 3-tensor weight-based interpretability.
- **OpenAI (2026):** [`openai/math`](https://github.com/openai/math) (commit `3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6`) — vendored Lean 4 tensor and geometric border-rank formalizations in `lean/Vendor/OAI/`.
