# eat-your-fiber

**Singular learning theory on—and at the boundary of—zero-loss fibers: Lean 4 kernel certificates paired with PyTorch empiricism.**

Different parameter vectors computing the *exact same function* can sit in valleys with macroscopically different local learning coefficients $(\lambda, m_{\log})$—and in higher-order multilinear models, the zero-loss fiber can disappear to infinity entirely while $\inf_\theta L(\theta) = 0$. Building on [residual-slt-in-a-haystack](https://github.com/bubskee/residual-slt-in-a-haystack) and [slt-bridge](https://github.com/bubskee/slt-bridge), this repository studies two complementary regimes side by side, aiming for macroscopic $\Delta\lambda$ phenomena that are simultaneously **kernel-certified in Lean 4** and **measured live in PyTorch**:

1. **Order-2 — Inside the zero-loss fiber (LoRA / Reduced-Rank / Self-Attention):** The rank-$\le k$ matrix locus is Zariski-closed, so every realizable target $M^*$ has a nonempty finite zero-loss fiber $\{BA = M^*\}$. Across that fiber, $(\lambda, m_{\log})$ undergoes an exact **plateau-to-parabola phase transition** (flat Aoyagi–Watanabe plateau for $q \le q_c$, exact parabola $\lambda_{\mathrm{AW}}^{\mathrm{even}} + \frac{1}{2}(q - q_c)^2$ for $q > q_c$—a rise of $\frac{1}{2}(q - q_c)^2$ above the plateau for even $m+n+k$, $\frac{1}{8}$ less for odd—placing standard LoRA initialization $q = k$ at the top of the parabola). In single-head self-attention (Frobenius weight-product loss), nonlinear composition creates a sharp **architectural coupling dichotomy**: joint `V+O` quadruples the supercritical jump to $2(q - H/2)_+^2$, whereas joint `Q+K` develops an all-orders $GL(q)$ gauge symmetry that **completely annihilates** the parabola (before multi-head transversality decouples both pairs). Both reappear at the supercritical tops inside a real pretrained 2-layer causal Transformer (subcritical points sit below theory at finite $\beta$), where iso-functional fiber surgery also separates plasticity from stability and splits late-stage LLC drops into functional change vs. pure fiber sliding.
2. **Order-3 — Boundary of the zero-loss fiber (Bilinear MLPs / 3-Tensors):** For width-$k$ bilinear models fitted entrywise to a 3-tensor, $T(A,B,C) = \sum_r a_r \otimes b_r \otimes c_r$ (a single-input bilinear MLP $x \mapsto W_{\mathrm{out}}((W_1 x) \odot (W_2 x))$ only sees the input-symmetrized part), the real rank-$\le k$ locus is **not closed**. When $T^*$ is a real limit of rank-$\le k$ tensors but $\mathrm{rk}(T^*) > k$ (e.g. $W$ at $k = 2$), $\inf_\theta L(\theta) = 0$ yet the finite zero-loss fiber is **empty**. Leveraging vendored 3-tensor border-rank formalizations from [`openai/math`](https://github.com/openai/math), we certify that under a Gaussian prior ($\ell_2$ weight decay), the canonical $W$-tensor forces a non-compact SLT universality class—**stretched-exponential free energy** $-\log Z_W^{(2)}(N) \asymp N^{1/7}$—strictly between realizable Watanabe $\Theta(\log N)$ and compact unrealizable $\Theta(N)$. $W$ sits on the measure-zero hyperdeterminant wall; on the **positive-measure** open set $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ (probability $1 - \pi/4 \approx 21.46\%$ for Gaussian $2\times 2\times 2$ targets), where no best rank-2 approximation exists and $\inf L = L_{\mathrm{wall}} > 0$ is never attained, a second fractional class appears on paper and in PyTorch: excess loss $\asymp \gamma^{3/4}$ and excess free energy $\asymp \beta^{1/4}$.

## Current highlights

### Track 1 — Order-3 Bilinear MLPs, Border-Rank Singularity & Stretched-Exponential SLT
- **`[LC]` Algebraic & analytic certificates ([`BorderRankSLT.lean`](lean/EatYourFiber/BorderRankSLT.lean)):**
  - Bridges width-$k$ bilinear MLPs $T(A, B, C) = \sum_{r=1}^k a_r \otimes b_r \otimes c_r$ directly to OpenAI's vendored `Tensor`, `tensorRank`, and `geometricBorderRank`.
  - Certifies $\mathrm{geometricBorderRank}(W) = 2 < 3 = \mathrm{tensorRank}(W)$ over every field $F$ for $W = e_0\otimes e_0\otimes e_1 + e_0\otimes e_1\otimes e_0 + e_1\otimes e_0\otimes e_0$ (using `card_le_geometricBorderRank_of_minor` from `openai/math` and 2×2 slice adjugate identities).
  - Proves the sharp quantitative escape law $\inf_{\|\theta\|_2 \le R} \|T(\theta) - W\|_F^2 \asymp R^{-12}$, achieved by the **centered finite-difference curve** $\tilde W_\varepsilon = \frac{1}{2\varepsilon}((e_0+\varepsilon e_1)^{\otimes 3} - (e_0-\varepsilon e_1)^{\otimes 3}) = W + \varepsilon^2 e_1^{\otimes 3}$ (beating the textbook one-sided curve $R^{-6}$ by $2\times$ in the exponent), the exact weight-decay law $\inf_\theta (\|T(\theta)-W\|_F^2 + \gamma \|\theta\|_2^2) \asymp \gamma^{6/7}$, and **stretched-exponential Bayesian free energy** $-\log Z_W^{(2)}(N) \asymp N^{1/7}$ contrasted against the **sharp width-3 codimension-8 RLCT bound** $-\log Z_W^{(3)}(N) \le 4\log N + C_3$ (`wPartitionZ_three_ge_poly_sharp`, proved via an explicit 10D rational fiber chart around the submersive realization $\frac{1}{2}(e_0+e_1)^{\otimes 3} - \frac{1}{2}(e_0-e_1)^{\otimes 3} - e_1^{\otimes 3}$).
- **`[empirical]` PyTorch verification ([`bilinear_border_rank.py`](experiments/bilinear_border_rank.py), [`bilinear_border_rank.md`](notes/bilinear_border_rank.md)):**
  - Random-start L-BFGS (no analytic seeding) spontaneously discovers the centered curve $\tilde W_\varepsilon$ (**16/16** at $\gamma \in \{10^{-2}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-4}\}$, **10/16** at $10^{-5}$; the random-start sweep with $\gamma$-continuation reaches `e111 = 0.999985`, `obj/pred = 1.0028`, virial `6.05` vs `6.00`, loss slope `0.851` vs `6/7`, norm² slope `-0.139` vs `-1/7`), plus a sharp width-3 first-order branch transition from the border-rank branch at high $\gamma$ to the rank-3 fiber at low $\gamma$.
  - Preconditioned SMC + HMC with an exact reversible Lie-group border-rank move up to $\beta = 10^6$ (independent cold-start chains agree on $\hat\lambda$ within $1.4\sigma$; width-3 $\mathbb{E}\lVert\theta\rVert_2^2$ differs by up to $3.7\sigma$) shows $\hat\lambda(\beta)$ plateauing flat at **`3.999 ± 0.061`** (mean ± SD over $\beta \ge 10^3$; $\lambda = 4$) for $k=3$, while for $k=2$ tracking the 4-scale Laplace prediction, asymptotically $2^{-10/7}\beta^{1/7} + \frac{27}{7}$ (`6.467 ± 0.070` vs. `6.522` from the finite-$\beta$ Laplace saddle, `6.531` from the asymptotic formula, at $\beta=10^6$; excess slope `0.1535` vs `1/7`; excess norm² slope `0.1415` vs `1/7`), yielding a **13.21-nat** Bayesian preference ($5.4 \times 10^5\times$ Bayes factor) for the *larger* width-3 model at $\beta = 10^6$.
  - A real 1-hidden-layer quadratic MLP $f(x) = W_{\mathrm{out}}(W_{\mathrm{in}} x)^{\odot 2}$ targeting $(2x_0 x_1, x_0^2)$ exhibits the same $\gamma^{6/7}$ vs. $\gamma^2$ laws and unregularized Adam weight explosion at $k=2$ vs. bounded convergence at $k=3$.
- **`[paper]` + `[empirical]` Beyond measure zero: the generic $\gamma^{3/4}$ / $\beta^{1/4}$ class ([`generic_border_rank.py`](experiments/generic_border_rank.py), [`generic_border_rank.md`](notes/generic_border_rank.md)):**
  - Cayley's hyperdeterminant gives $\operatorname{Det}(T(\theta)) = (\det A\,\det B\,\det C)^2 \gtrsim R^{-6}$ for width-2 $T(\theta)$ near a smooth wall point; on $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ the wall multiplier is strictly positive, so $\Delta L \asymp R^{-6}$ (vs. $R^{-12}$ on $W$). A 500,000-draw Monte Carlo measures $P(\operatorname{Det} < 0) = $ `0.21461 ± 0.00058` vs. Bergqvist's exact $1 - \pi/4 = 0.21460$.
  - Random-start L-BFGS with $\gamma$-continuation on 6 generic $\operatorname{Det} < 0$ draws: $\Delta L^*$ slope **`0.7476 ± 0.0015`** (theory $3/4$), norm² slope **`-0.2496 ± 0.0014`** ($-1/4$), virial **`3.01`** ($3$); $\operatorname{Det} > 0$ draws and width $k=3$ revert to $\gamma^{2}$ (`1.997`, `2.000`).
  - SMC + HMC with exact $\mathrm{GL}(2)^3$ Lie-group moves to $\beta = 10^6$: $\hat\lambda_{\mathrm{exc}} - \frac{33}{8}$ slope **`0.2529`** and $\mathbb{E}\lVert\theta\rVert_2^2 - \frac{15}{4}$ slope **`0.2494`** (theory $1/4$); the $k=2$ excess free energy over $\beta L_{\mathrm{wall}}$ is `167.01` nats (Laplace `166.12`) vs. `65.65` for $k=3$, whose $\hat\lambda$ sits at `4.024 ± 0.061` (mean ± SD over $\beta \ge 10^3$; `3.975 ± 0.042` at $10^6$).
  - Degeneration becomes common in higher dimensions: the L-BFGS detector flags **`77.5%`, `85%`, `90%`** of 40 Gaussian $4\times 4\times 4$ targets at $k = 4, 5, 6$, and 14/20 generic $d = 4 \to 4$ quadratic tasks for a real width-4 Bilinear MLP (norm² slope `-0.236`). SwiGLU splits: on bilinear targets it escapes via gate linearization or pairs neurons via $\operatorname{SiLU}(z) - \operatorname{SiLU}(-z) = z$ (SwiGLU width 12 realizes at norm² ratio `1.99999` to Bilinear width 6, max pair cosine $1 - 7\times 10^{-12}$); on one SwiGLU-native teacher its norms stay bounded.

  - **2D fiber surgery:** 25 checkpoints computing the same function to float64 precision ($\max\lvert\Delta\mathrm{logit}\rvert = 1.5\times 10^{-14}$) reproduce both supercritical parabolas, (0,8) at `61.65 ± 0.50` and (8,0) at `61.98 ± 0.40` (theory $62$), and off-axis mutual exclusivity (`55.09–56.14` vs. $56$). The plateau sits `0.4–4.9` below $54$, the usual finite-$\beta$ offset.
  - **Pre-LN breaks $p \leftrightarrow q$ symmetry:** mean-centering puts $\{\mathbf{1}_d u^\top\}$ in the Fisher kernel (exactly $12$ zero eigenvalues vs. $0$ under Pre-RMSNorm), predicting tops $57$ vs. $61$; MALA gives `56.38 ± 0.72` vs. `60.07 ± 0.63`.
  - **Same function, different futures:** fine-tuning function-matched Task-A checkpoints on Task B (5 seeds), (0,0) stays near rank 2 and forgets less (Task-A KL `0.385` vs. `0.464`), while (8,0) reaches $2.2\times$ the Task-B recall of (0,0) under SGD (`0.473` vs. `0.211`); on noisy small-sample Task B, (0,0) has a $2.6$–$5.3\times$ smaller overfitting gap (`+0.126` SGD, `+0.081` Adam vs. `+0.333–0.433`).
  - **LLC drops that are not development:** after Task-A KL has converged, `LoRA + AdamW` loses $\Delta\hat\lambda = $ **`-10.79 ± 0.83`**, of which `-10.28 ± 1.07` (**$95.3\% \pm 6.4$** points, delta-method SE) is iso-functional fiber sliding and `-0.51 ± 0.69` is the change in balanced-checkpoint LLC (Task-A KL `1.5e-8 → 3.5e-8`); `SGD+WD` instead shows a large balanced-LLC drop (`-14.83 ± 0.87`) as its residual `dW_err` shrinks `2.3e-2 → 4.5e-3`.
  - **Attention dichotomy and multi-head reversal:** at 1 head, `V+O` reaches `45.10 ± 0.40` ($96$ Hessian eigenvalues above $\gamma$) while `Q+K` is held at `38.48 ± 0.37` by the $16$-dim $GL(4)$ gauge ($80$); at 2 and 4 heads both land on $48$ (`47.98`/`47.86`, `47.95`/`48.47`; $96$).

## Scope, assumptions & honest limitations

1. **Non-compact prior vs. Watanabe's compact parameter space (Track 1):** Standard SLT assumes compact prior support $\Theta \subset \mathbb{R}^P$; on any compact ball $\|\theta\|_2 \le R$, width-2 $W$-tensor fitting is strictly unrealizable ($L^*(R) \asymp R^{-12} > 0$) with linear free energy $-\log Z \sim N L^*(R)$. The stretched-exponential class $-\log Z_W^{(2)}(N) \asymp N^{1/7}$ specifically requires a **non-compact Gaussian prior** ($\ell_2$ weight decay); general tails $e^{-c R^\alpha}$ give $N^{\alpha/(12+\alpha)}$, and heavy-tailed priors $R^{-\nu}$ yield prior-dependent $\Theta(\log N)$ scaling (see [`notes/bilinear_border_rank.md`](notes/bilinear_border_rank.md) §2.4).
2. **Formalization boundaries on Order-2 fibers (Track 2):**
   - Complete $(r^*, p, q)$ RRR fiber stratification for arbitrary invertible active block $D \in GL(r^*, \mathbb{R})$ (`RRRFiberStratification.lean`) and single-head `Q+K` flat cancellation for arbitrary orthogonal bases $U, V \in O(d)$ (`AttentionLoRA.lean`) are **`[LC]`**.
   - Non-orthogonal `V+O` row-space overlap (`q_eff = rank([A_{O,0}; A_{V,0}])`), multi-head decoupling ($n_{\mathrm{heads}} \ge 2$), and nonlinear MLP pullback invariance through a non-degenerate Gauss-Newton Fisher $\mathcal{H} \succ 0$ remain `[paper]` + `[empirical]` ([`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md)).
3. **Low-sample noisy fine-tuning nuance:** When finite noisy training data ($S = 48, \sigma = 0.25$) excites all $H$ LoRA ranks into fitting noise, there is no inactive zero-loss fiber ($k_{\mathrm{eff}} = 0$) and moderate decoupled AdamW shrinks $\|A_{\perp B}\|_F$ without improving test MSE; strong AdamW, coupled SGD+WD, or subcritical rank-$2$ initialization cut test MSE by $2\text{--}8\times$. Moreover, raw centered empirical LLC turns strongly negative (`-314.7`) at the generalizing rank-2 saddle, illustrating why functional KL LLC is essential ([`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md) §2.2).
4. **Finite-$\beta$ estimation & sampler choice:** Across all empirical sweeps, Metropolis-adjusted samplers (MALA / preconditioned MALA / SMC+HMC) are used because unadjusted SGLD incurs severe upward discretization bias on stiff supercritical directions (`36.81` to `54.54` vs. `32.00` on GELU MLP; [`notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md`](notes/lora_nonlinear_fiber.md) §1.3). On singular subcritical plateaus ($q < q_c$), finite-$\beta$ MALA retains the expected negative $O(1/\log(\beta/\gamma))$ offset that closes monotonically as $\beta/\gamma$ grows.
5. **Generic border-rank class is `[paper]` + `[empirical]`, and SwiGLU has two regimes (Track 1):** Only the $W$-tensor class is Lean-certified; the local hyperdeterminant bound $\operatorname{Det}(T(\theta)) \gtrsim R^{-6}$ and the $\gamma^{3/4}$ / $\beta^{1/4}$ laws are not. The $77.5\text{--}90\%$ rates at $d = 4$ come from an L-BFGS degeneracy detector on 40 targets per $(d,k)$, not an exact classification. On a bilinear target ($d = 4 \to 4$; Bilinear width 4 fails, width 6 realizes), under-width SwiGLU escapes by gate linearization ($W_1 \to 0$; predicted $\mathrm{MSE}^* \asymp \gamma^{2/3}$, virial $2$): at width 6 we measured virial `2.02` but MSE slope `0.742`, so the exponent is only roughly confirmed. On one SwiGLU-native $d = 3$ teacher, widths 2–4 keep bounded norms (norm² $\le 33.3$) across $\gamma \in [10^{-5}, 10^{-1}]$, suggesting the escape comes from bilinear/quadratic sub-circuits rather than SwiGLU per se (one teacher tested) ([`notes/generic_border_rank.md`](notes/generic_border_rank.md) §3.4).
6. **Pre-LN vs. Pre-RMSNorm (Track 2):** Pre-LN's mean-centering puts the rank-$d$ family $\mathbf{1}_d u^\top$ in the Fisher kernel of residual-stream writes, dropping $M_{\mathrm{eff}}$ to $d - 1$ and breaking $p \leftrightarrow q$ symmetry; Pre-RMSNorm has no such kernel and stays symmetric. The derivation is `[paper]`; it was checked on one $3{,}240$-parameter Pre-LN twin that only partly learned the pretraining task (recall `0.46`, vs. `1.00` for the Pre-RMSNorm model).
7. **Optimizer dependence of fiber locking (Track 2, $d_{\mathrm{model}} = 12$ Transformer, 5 seeds):** From the exact $(0,0)$ point both SGD and Adam stay near rank 2. From a realistic near-plateau start $(0,0) + 10^{-3}$ ($\max\lvert\Delta\mathrm{logit}\rvert = 6.2\times 10^{-4}$), SGD stays locked (recall `0.253`, update effective rank `2.41`), but Adam's $1/\sqrt{v}$ normalization breaks the saddle (`0.595`, `3.53`), matching the supercritical checkpoints, so the plasticity–stability split largely washes out under Adam on non-directional tasks. With $\mathrm{wd} = 0$, LoRA+ ($\eta_B = 12\,\eta_A$) ends deeper in the $A$-supercritical cone than equal-LR Adam ($(p_{\mathrm{eff}}, q_{\mathrm{eff}}) = (0.00, 6.73)$, $\hat\lambda = $ `61.47 ± 0.46` vs. $(0.24, 5.57)$, `58.44 ± 0.53`), because the small $\eta_A$ freezes $A_{\mathrm{in}}$ at its full-rank init. Extrapolation to large Transformers with stochastic schedules is `[conjectural]`.

## Relation to SLT & ML literature

- **Watanabe (2009, 2018):** *Algebraic Geometry and Statistical Learning Theory* (CUP) and *Mathematical Theory of Bayesian Statistics* (CRC) — foundational SLT free-energy asymptotics, RLCTs $(\lambda, m)$, and WBIC.
- **Aoyagi & Watanabe (2005):** *"Stochastic complexities of reduced rank regression in Bayesian estimation,"* *Neural Networks* 18(7): 924–933 — Theorem 1 gives the exact RLCTs of matrix factorization, here unconditionally mechanized in Lean 4 via elementary projective blowups (`aoyagi_watanabe_rank0`).
- **Aoyagi (2024):** *"Consideration on the learning efficiency of multiple-layered neural networks with linear units,"* *Neural Networks* — global RLCTs of deep linear networks.
- **Lau, Furman, Wang, Murfet & Wei (2025):** *"The Local Learning Coefficient: A Singularity-Aware Complexity Measure,"* AISTATS 2025 ([`arXiv:2308.12108`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12108)) and **Furman & Lau (2024)** ([`arXiv:2402.03698`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03698)) — local learning coefficient theory and scalable estimation on deep linear and neural networks; our Track 2 maps exact local LLC phase transitions *within* a fixed-function zero-loss fiber.
- **Treisman (2025/2026):** [`ztreisman/arc-llc`](https://github.com/ztreisman/arc-llc) — arc-space computation of local RLCTs for reduced-rank regression via Smith normal form over $\mathbb{C}[[t]]$ and Docampo's formula, proposing the $(r^*, a, b)$ local RLCT formula as a candidate solution to **MAIS-O70**; our [`RRRFiberStratification.lean`](lean/EatYourFiber/RRRFiberStratification.lean) certifies this formula unconditionally over $\mathbb{R}$ in Lean 4 and proves its equivalence to minimizing irreducible fiber-component codimensions.
- **Hoogland, Wang, Farrugia-Roberts, Carroll, Wei & Murfet (2024/2025):** *"Loss Landscape Degeneracy and Stagewise Development in Transformers,"* to appear in TMLR ([`arXiv:2402.02364`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02364); v1 titled *"The Developmental Landscape of In-Context Learning"*) — reads developmental stages off LLC curves; our late-stage $\Delta\hat\lambda$ decomposition into functional change vs. iso-functional fiber sliding is a check one can run before attributing an LLC drop to a stage.
- **Hu et al. (2022):** *"LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models,"* ICLR 2022 ([`arXiv:2106.09685`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)) — standard asymmetric $(B=0, A \sim \mathcal{N})$ initialization sits at the supercritical top $q = k$.
- **Hayou, Ghosh & Yu (2024):** *"LoRA+: Efficient Low Rank Adaptation of Large Models,"* ICML 2024, PMLR 235: 17783–17806 ([`arXiv:2402.12354`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12354)) — separate learning rates $\eta_B > \eta_A$; in our Transformer runs ($\mathrm{wd} = 0$) this leaves LoRA+ deeper in the $A$-supercritical cone.
- **de Silva & Lim (2008):** *"Tensor rank and the ill-posedness of the best low-rank approximation problem,"* *SIAM J. Matrix Anal. Appl.* 30(3): 1084–1127 ([`arXiv:math/0607647`](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607647)) — border rank, non-closedness of the rank-$\le k$ tensor locus, and a positive-volume set of real $2\times 2\times 2$ tensors with no best rank-2 approximation; the origin of our generic $\operatorname{Det}(T^*) < 0$ class.
- **Bergqvist (2013):** *"Exact probabilities for typical ranks of $2\times 2\times 2$ and $3\times 3\times 2$ tensors,"* *Linear Algebra Appl.* 438(2): 663–667 — a real Gaussian $2\times 2\times 2$ tensor has rank 3 with probability exactly $1 - \pi/4$, the measure of our generic border-rank class.
- **Sharkey (2023)** ([`arXiv:2305.03452`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03452)) & **Pearce, Dooms, Rigg, Oramas & Sharkey (2024)** ([`arXiv:2410.08417`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08417)) — bilinear MLPs and 3-tensor weight-based interpretability.
- **OpenAI (2026):** [`openai/math`](https://github.com/openai/math) (commit `3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6`) — vendored Lean 4 tensor and geometric border-rank formalizations in `lean/Vendor/OAI/`.
